ABOUT OUR THINKING

Is a Criminal Born or Made?

No human being is simply defined by their past. A person's life is influenced by family, society, education, environment and circumstances.

Understanding the reasons behind crime does not mean supporting crime. It means understanding human behaviour and searching for ways to prevent crime.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to understand the social, psychological and environmental factors associated with crime.

We aim to spread awareness, support victims' rights, encourage equal opportunities and promote a safer and more responsible society.

WHY DO PEOPLE COMMIT CRIMES?

Crime does not have one single cause. Many factors may influence human behaviour.

01. Family Environment

Family conflict, neglect and lack of support may affect a person's development.

02. Society

Social exclusion, discrimination and peer pressure may influence behaviour.

03. Poverty and Education

Lack of opportunities and education can create difficult circumstances.

04. Life Experiences

Stress, trauma and difficult experiences may influence a person's choices.

Real Stories, Real Lessons

Indian Case Study

Read Full Case Study →

The Nirbhaya case of 2012 became an important turning point in India's discussion about women's safety, justice and legal reforms.

Key Learning: The case highlighted the importance of women's safety, equality, accountability and timely justice.

Key Learning: Understanding the background of a crime is important for prevention, justice and social awareness.

International Case Study

The Amanda Knox case in Italy raised important questions about criminal investigations, evidence and the right to a fair trial. In 2015, Italy's highest court definitively acquitted Amanda Knox of Meredith Kercher's murder.

Key Learning: Reliable evidence, fair investigations, responsible media reporting and legal rights are essential for justice.

Key Learning: Crime prevention requires education, social support, accountability and a fair justice system.

HOW CAN WE PREVENT CRIME?

Understanding the causes of crime can help society find better solutions.

Education

Education creates awareness and better opportunities.

Family Support

A supportive family environment can help children grow positively.

Equal Opportunities

Equal access to jobs and education can reduce social disadvantages.

Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation can help individuals rebuild their lives.

Crime Prevention & Awareness

Crime prevention is a shared responsibility. Education, awareness, social support and equal opportunities can help build a safer society.

Education

Education helps people understand their rights, responsibilities and laws.

Community Support

Supportive families and communities can help people overcome difficult situations.

Legal Awareness

Knowledge of laws and legal rights helps people seek justice.

Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation can help people rebuild their lives and return to society.

Victims' Rights & Support

Every victim deserves dignity, safety, support and access to justice. Society must respect their rights and help them rebuild their lives.

Right to Safety

Victims deserve protection from further harm and intimidation.

Right to Justice

Victims should have access to a fair legal process.

Emotional Support

Counselling and family support can help victims recover.
